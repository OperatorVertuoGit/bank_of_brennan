# Owner inbox

Anything an agent cannot decide lands in `data/bus/owner/inbox/` and is summarized here
by whoever runs the daily pass. Decisions only the owner makes:

- Sending a quote, an invoice, or any email to a client.
- Approving an on-site visit, work at height, or a confined-space job.
- Approving a price outside `data/registry/pricing.json`.
- Publishing anything to the website.
- Permission questions: spraying, cleaning or disassembling a client's part.
- Intellectual-property ambiguity on a reverse-engineering request.
- Anything past 60 days in accounts receivable.
